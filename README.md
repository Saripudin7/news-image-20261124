One 1024×1024 infographic summing up the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**,
as reported on that day. Six stories, six regions and fields:

1. **Asia · Markets (24 Nov)** — Asian stocks surge (Nikkei +4.5%, Kospi +7.5%, Taiwan +9.1%)
   after Nvidia jumped 31.5% on Jensen Huang's return as CEO. *Reuters*
2. **Libya · War at sea (attack 22 Nov, reported 23 Nov)** — Ukraine says seven sea drones sank
   the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi; cargo ship MV Angara also hit. *Reuters, AP, CNN*
3. **Turkey · Climate (23 Nov)** — COP31 talks collapse with no deal after governments reject
   a draft deal with no mention of fossil fuels. *Reuters, Channel NewsAsia*
4. **England · Safety (22 Nov)** — 12,000 fans evacuated from the Don Toliver concert in
   Manchester after an unknown substance was released into the crowd. *The Guardian, AP*
5. **China · Justice (reported 24 Nov)** — A state official is sentenced to death for leaking
   state secrets to a foreign country. *Times of India*
6. **Sport · Football (24 Nov)** — The Court of Arbitration for Sport rules on Senegal's
   Africa Cup of Nations title appeal. *Reuters*

Each panel shows the date its event happened or was first reported.

## How to run

```sh
python3 render.py          # redraw entry.png with Pillow (reference only)
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # copies entry.png to out/entry.png
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library and no network.
